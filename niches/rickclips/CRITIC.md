# Niche critic additions: rickclips (on top of docs/AUTOPILOT.md)

- Facts: years, names, sums of money and places of historical cons/inventions — verify twice; famous stories often have
  embellished versions (e.g. Lustig "sold the tower twice": the second sale is reported but less documented → "reportedly").
- Visuals: era objects (no modern items before their invention), correct landmarks (Eiffel Tower shape), currency and sign language
  of the place; mechanisms in the cutaway must work physically as narrated (water levels, siphon, levers, gears).
- Forbidden: real people shown committing crimes they didn't commit; gore; real brand logos.
